SIMPLE RULES. BIG MOMENTS.

One number can change everything.

0–6
Batter picks 0 to 6

Zero is a special no-run choice available only to the batter.

1–6
Bowler picks 1 to 6

Matching numbers mean a wicket. Different numbers score the batter's choice.

BEFORE THE FIRST BALL

How to play

01

Choose together.
Both players lock in a number before either choice is revealed.

02

Score your number.
If the numbers differ, the batter scores the number they picked.

03

Matching numbers = OUT.
In Quick Play, one wicket ends the innings. Custom and tournament formats are designed for multiple batters.

04

Remember the zero.
Only the batter can choose 0. It scores no runs and cannot be a dismissal.
